# ECONOMIC CALENDAR

## Purpose
Track important economic events that may affect our markets.

## Events to Monitor
- US inflation (CPI)
- US employment reports
- Federal Reserve interest rate decisions
- GDP releases
- Central bank announcements
- Major crypto events
- Exchange maintenance and outages

## Event Rules
1. Record the event name and source.
2. Record the release date and time in UTC.
3. Convert the time to my local timezone when needed.
4. Flag high-impact events.
5. Pause new entries 30 minutes before major releases.
6. Resume only after checking market conditions.

## Data Sources
- Official economic calendars
- Federal Reserve
- US Bureau of Labor Statistics
- Official exchange announcements

## Important
Never invent event dates or release times.
Always verify event information from an official source.
