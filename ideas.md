# STRATEGY IDEAS

## Purpose
Store potential trading strategies for research and testing.

## Strategy Families
1. Trend following
2. Mean reversion
3. Funding and basis carry
4. Relative value
5. Volatility breakout
6. Cross-sectional momentum
7. Event-driven strategies

## Strategy Evaluation
For every idea, record:
- Strategy name
- Market
- Entry conditions
- Exit conditions
- Stop-loss rules
- Position sizing
- Trading fees
- Slippage assumptions
- Backtest results
- Out-of-sample results
- Reasons for failure

## Rules
- Never trade an untested idea.
- Never assume profitability.
- Include realistic trading costs.
- Record failed experiments.
- Paper trade before considering live trading.
